Who We Are

A quiet, loving place where people of every walk of life can receive prayer — and meet Jesus, the same yesterday, today and forever.

i.

Our Mission

Healing Rooms host the Presence of Jesus, our Healer, to see people healed, set free, to live a life the Lord has planned for them demonstrating the Kingdom of God.

ii.

Our Vision

A loving and safe environment where people of all walks receive prayer for physical, mental, and emotional afflictions. We follow the example of Jesus Christ, featured in the Bible, who is the same yesterday, today and forever.

A Place of Encounter

Where people meet the Healer

Every Thursday evening, our trained prayer teams welcome people from all walks of life — no matter their background, belief, or burden. Whether you’re carrying physical pain, emotional weight, or mental distress, you are welcome here.

We believe that Jesus is the same yesterday, today, and forever — and His compassion still moves to heal.

Questions

Frequently asked

New to healing rooms? Here are answers to the questions people ask most before their first visit.

When is Wells of Healing open?

Every Thursday evening from 7:00 to 8:30 PM in Room 104 at 11295 Mellis Drive, Richmond, BC (Fraserview MB Church).

Do I need an appointment?

No. We’re walk-in only — no appointment is necessary. Simply arrive any Thursday between 7:00 and 8:30 PM.

Is there a cost for prayer?

No. Prayer is always free of charge. Freely we have received, freely we give.

Do I have to be a Christian or attend a church?

No. People from all walks of life are welcome, no matter their background, belief, or burden. We provide a safe, loving environment for everyone seeking prayer.

What can I receive prayer for?

You can receive prayer for physical needs such as pain, illness, and injury; mental burdens such as anxiety and depression; and emotional wounds such as grief, trauma, and fear.

What happens when I arrive?

You’ll be welcomed and a trained prayer team will pray with you personally, following the example of Jesus Christ, in a safe and loving environment. Come as you are.

Which entrance do I use?

Enter through the back door of Fraserview MB Church at 11295 Mellis Drive, Richmond — the same entrance as the Canadian Star Secondary School — and come to Room 104. Open in Google Maps.

Is Wells of Healing part of a larger ministry?

Yes. Wells of Healing is affiliated with the International Association of Healing Rooms (IAHR).
